What income counts for referencing?

PAYE: Usually gross verified salary / earnings before tax.

Self-employed: Often annual trading profit after allowable business expenses, before personal tax and NI.

Other income: Agency discretion applies. Only include income your referencing provider accepts.

BONUSES AND WAYS TO SAVE FOR YOUR DEPOSIT

Extra help before buying

Lifetime ISA (LISA)

Eligible savers aged 18–39 can open a LISA and receive a 25% government bonus on eligible contributions up to £4,000 per tax year. To use it without a withdrawal penalty for your first home, your property must cost £450,000 or less and your first contribution must have been made at least 12 months before purchase. Other rules apply.

See LISA rules ↗
First-time buyer Stamp Duty relief

In England, if every purchaser qualifies and the property costs at most £500,000, Stamp Duty is 0% up to £300,000 and 5% on the amount from £300,000–£500,000. Above £500,000, standard SDLT bands apply instead. For shared ownership, tax treatment is more complex.

See Stamp Duty rules ↗
Rent to Buy / London Living Rent

These schemes help renters build up savings; they do not directly reduce today's mortgage. Rent to Buy usually offers 20% below local market rent outside London. London Living Rent is a separate London scheme with its own income and eligibility limits.

Rent to Buy ↗ · London Living Rent ↗
Existing Help to Buy ISA

Can't be opened now, but existing eligible accounts may still provide a bonus of up to £3,000. The former England Help to Buy equity loan scheme is closed to new applicants. Other UK nations have different programmes.

Help to Buy ISA ↗

What you need to do next

  1. Check which route and properties are actually available where you want to live.
  2. Estimate your deposit, Stamp Duty, legal fees, mortgage and ongoing costs.
  3. For PAYE, gather payslips and employment details; for self-employment, gather SA302s, tax year overviews and accounts. Lenders set their own evidence requirements.
  4. Check your credit file, commitments and monthly budget; consider an FCA-authorised mortgage adviser.
  5. Get a mortgage Agreement in Principle, then find a property and arrange legal advice before committing.

THE FIRST-TIMER QUICK GUIDE

Who actually does each check?

Letting agent

Collects your application and documents, may request references and coordinates the decision.

Referencing company

May verify income, contact employers/landlords and produce a report for the agent.

Landlord

Chooses whether to accept you and remains responsible for Right to Rent unless responsibility is transferred to an agent in writing.

HMRC / employer / accountant

Provide or confirm financial evidence. HMRC does not normally decide whether you pass a tenant reference check.

What if you don't have the usual paperwork?

Tell the agent early. Ask whether they will accept alternative income proof, a previous landlord's note, an accountant's letter, savings evidence or a guarantor. None is an automatic substitute: acceptance is up to the provider.
