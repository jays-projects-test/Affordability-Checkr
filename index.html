Income Tax bands

Personal Allowance£12,570 · 0%
Basic rate · next £37,70020%
Higher rate · to £125,140 taxable40%

The Personal Allowance reduces by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000, reaching zero at £125,140.

National Insurance

PAYE · above £50,2702%
Self-employed Class 4 · £12,570–£50,2706%
Class 4 · above £50,2702%

Class 2 NI is normally treated as paid once profits reach the relevant threshold; voluntary Class 2 isn't included.

What income counts for referencing?

PAYE: Usually gross verified salary / earnings before tax.

Self-employed: Often annual trading profit after allowable business expenses, before personal tax and NI.

Other income: Agency discretion applies. Only include income your referencing provider accepts.
