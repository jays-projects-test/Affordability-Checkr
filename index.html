What income counts for referencing?

PAYE: Usually gross verified salary / earnings before tax.

Self-employed: Often annual trading profit after allowable business expenses, before personal tax and NI.

Other income: Agency discretion applies. Only include income your referencing provider accepts.

THE FIRST-TIMER QUICK GUIDE

Who actually does each check?

Letting agent

Collects your application and documents, may request references and coordinates the decision.

Referencing company

May verify income, contact employers/landlords and produce a report for the agent.

Landlord

Chooses whether to accept you and remains responsible for Right to Rent unless responsibility is transferred to an agent in writing.

HMRC / employer / accountant

Provide or confirm financial evidence. HMRC does not normally decide whether you pass a tenant reference check.

What if you don't have the usual paperwork?

Tell the agent early. Ask whether they will accept alternative income proof, a previous landlord's note, an accountant's letter, savings evidence or a guarantor. None is an automatic substitute: acceptance is up to the provider.
